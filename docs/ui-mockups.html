Surface 04 · admin

Review queue — only the worst records, side by side

Two-tier policy in action: everything publishes, but very-low-confidence or sanity-check-failing records land here for priority correction. Every fix becomes a few-shot example that makes the extractor sharper — the corpus competitors can't copy.
